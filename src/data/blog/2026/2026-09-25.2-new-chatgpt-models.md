# AI Models: OpenAI GPT 5.6 comparison with GPT 6

OpenAI has [recently released GPT 6](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/), an upgrade to their GPT 5.6 models.

Personally I prefer the Anthropic Opus models for heavy-duty AI tasks, but end up using the Sol and Luna models for heaps of day to day work. Luna is great for simple and fast low level work - e.g. git commands, cli commands, curl commands, powershell and bash commands. If I know what I want, just do it. I use Sol as a _cheaper_ replacement for Opus tasks.

## OpenAI Pricing

OpenAI states in their announcement blog:

GPT‑6 API pricing

| Model                     | Input         | Output        | Price reduction |
| ------------------------- | ------------- | ------------- | --------------- |
| GPT‑5.6 Sol → GPT‑6 Sol   | $4 → $2       | $20 → $10     | **50% cheaper** |
| GPT‑5.6 Luna → GPT‑6 Luna | $0.20 → $0.10 | $1.20 → $0.50 | **50% cheaper** |

## Final thoughts

One of the more interesting parts of these comparisons is that I think I'll move off MEDIUM and onto HIGH or XHIGH. The cost is minimal but _feels like_ it could get more intelligence. So a mix of extra (cheap) cost for intelligence and speed might be worth it.
