# AI Models: Anthropic Opus 5.5 comparison with Opus 5

Anthropic has [recently released Opus 5.5](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5), an upgrade to their Opus 5 models.

I've been using Opus 5 for a number of months for various _tough_ engineering problems. I also use OpenAI's ChatGPT 5.5 Sol, Terra and Luna. _Personally I don't like supporting ChatGPT or xAI, but for work we've been restricted to Anthropic and ChatGPT models._

With the release of Opus 5.5, I wanted to compare price vs "intelligence" based off a favourite website I used to pick my AI models.

All the data/charts are taken from the lovely website [Artifical Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/?releases=claude-opus-5-5%2Cclaude-opus-5%2Cclaude-sonnet-5#release-comparison-tabs).

## Opus 5 (Medium) vs Opus 5.5 (Medium)

![Artifical Analysis comparison chart](@/assets/images/2026-09-25.1/aa-opus-cost-chat-2026-09-25.png)

![Opus 5 comparison chart](@/assets/images/2026-09-25.1/opus-5-medium.png)

![Opus 5.5 comparison chart](@/assets/images/2026-09-25.1/opus-5-5-medium.png)

- [Opus 5 (Medium)](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/releases/claude-opus-5)
  - Cost: $2.19
  - Intelligence: 45
  - Speed: 58 t/s
- [Opus 5.5 (Medium)](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/releases/claude-opus-5-5)
  - Cost: $1.34
  - Intelligence: 51
  - Speed: 80 t/s

Opus 5.5 (Medium) is **38.8% CHEAPER** than Opus 5 (Medium) and also **13.3% MORE INTELLIGENT** and **37.9% FASTER** than Opus 5 (Medium).

## Final Thoughts

Not sure what the "with fallback" in the images mean, but the new Opus 5.5 (Medium) falls inside the "Attractive Quadrant" which is a nice target to aim for.

Time to move over to Opus 5.5 for all my AI model needs and experiment.

Happy coding!
